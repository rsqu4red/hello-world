# Bildprompts für Visualisierungen – Wochenendhaus Flurstück 7348, Bammental

Für **Nano Banana (Gemini Image)**, Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram o. ä. Englische Prompts funktionieren bei allen Modellen am zuverlässigsten; deutsche Kurzfassung jeweils darunter.

## So gehst du vor

1. **Referenzbilder mitgeben** (Nano Banana / Gemini erlaubt mehrere Bilder pro Prompt):
   - Bild 1: dein Drohnenfoto mit dem weißen Baufenster
   - Bild 2 + 3: die beiden Cabin-Referenzen (Lärchenschalung, Stehfalzdach, Schiebetür)
   - Bild 4: die Isometrie bzw. die Ansichten aus der Planmappe (Blatt 4/6) – das zwingt das Modell auf die richtigen Proportionen (7,20 × 5,20 m, 45°-Dach, Firsthöhe 5,30 m)
2. Immer die **Fixdaten** anhängen (Block ganz unten), sonst werden die Häuser zu groß, zu flach oder bekommen Gauben.
3. Pro Motiv 4–8 Varianten generieren, die beste auswählen, dann mit „keep everything, only change …“ verfeinern.
4. Für die Gemeinde reichen 3 Bilder: (A) Einfügung ins Drohnenfoto, (B) Ansicht von der Wiese auf Augenhöhe, (C) Nordseite vom Grenzweg. Optional (D) Terrasse abends und (E) Innenraum.

---

## Prompt A – Einfügung in das Drohnenfoto (wichtigstes Bild)

> Photo editing task. Use the attached drone photograph of a meadow in front of an autumn forest as the base image. Inside the white rectangle on the lawn, place a small modern timber cabin, exactly filling the rectangle's footprint (7.2 m × 5.2 m) and aligned with its edges. Remove the white rectangle. The cabin: single storey, steep 45° gable roof with the ridge running along the long side, ridge height 5.3 m, roof clad in matte anthracite standing-seam metal, very short roof overhangs (40 cm at the eaves, 30 cm at the gables), walls clad in untreated horizontal larch boards weathered to silver grey, no visible foundation, the building sits 30 cm above the grass on hidden point footings. On the side facing the camera (the open meadow) there is a large floor-to-ceiling sliding glass door and, beside it, a sliding shutter of vertical larch slats. A low timber deck, 3 m deep, runs along the entire glazed side at floor level. The gable ends are calm with one small window each. No dormers, no chimney, no balcony, no fence, no cars. Match the lighting of the photo exactly: low warm afternoon autumn sun from the left, long soft shadows on the grass, slight haze. Keep the excavator, the yellow construction trailer, the forest and everything else in the photo unchanged. Photorealistic, seamless integration, correct perspective from the drone's viewpoint, high detail.

**Deutsch (Kurz):** Bearbeite das Drohnenfoto: Setze in das weiße Rechteck eine kleine moderne Holzhütte (7,20 × 5,20 m, Satteldach 45°, First 5,30 m, anthrazit Stehfalz, silbergraue Lärche horizontal, große Schiebeverglasung + Holz-Schiebeladen zur Wiese, 3 m tiefe Holzterrasse). Weißes Rechteck entfernen, Licht und Schatten des Fotos übernehmen, alles andere unverändert lassen.

**Verfeinerung, falls nötig:** „Keep the image, only make the cabin smaller so it fits the rectangle exactly; roof steeper (45°); remove the chimney/dormer; make the wood more silver-grey.“

## Prompt B – Augenhöhe von der Wiese (Südwest), Hauptbild „Einfügung ins Ortsbild“

> Architectural photograph, eye level, taken from the meadow in the late afternoon in autumn. A small modern weekend cabin at the edge of a deciduous forest with orange and yellow autumn foliage (beech, oak) in the background, on a gently sloping meadow with wild grass. The cabin is a simple archetypal house shape: 7.2 m long, 5.2 m wide, single storey, steep 45° gable roof, ridge 5.3 m above the floor, matte dark grey standing-seam metal roof with narrow seams, very short overhangs, horizontal larch cladding weathered to silver grey with open joints. The long south side facing the camera has a floor-to-ceiling sliding glass door, two panes, and next to it a wide sliding shutter made of vertical larch slats parked in front of a smaller window. A low larch deck, 3 m deep, the full length of the house, only 30 cm above the grass, no railing. Two small wooden steps. Warm light inside, a simple wooden chair on the deck. Calm, understated, Scandinavian-Japanese minimalism, in the style of Go Logic's "Little House on the Ferry". Realistic materials, 35 mm lens, soft golden light from the side, slight mist between the trees. No people, no cars, no fence, no dormers, no chimney.

**Deutsch (Kurz):** Architekturfoto auf Augenhöhe: kleine moderne Wochenendhütte am Waldrand mit Herbstlaub, 7,20 × 5,20 m, Satteldach 45°, dunkelgraues Stehfalzblech, silbergraue Lärche, Schiebeverglasung + Lärchen-Schiebeladen, flache Holzterrasse ohne Geländer, warmes Abendlicht, ruhig, minimalistisch.

## Prompt C – Nordseite vom Grenzweg (ruhige Eingangsfassade)

> Architectural photograph from a narrow gravel forest lane, looking at the closed north side of a small modern timber cabin standing about 20 m back on a meadow, with tall autumn forest behind and to the right. The facade is calm: horizontal silver-grey larch boards, a single wooden entrance door, one horizontal kitchen window with a dark frame, one small square bathroom window, a small dark wall lamp. Steep 45° gable roof in matte anthracite standing-seam metal, ridge parallel to the lane, short overhangs, a thin dark gutter and one downpipe leading into a rain barrel/cistern. A gravel footpath from the lane to the door, a gravel-grass parking space near the lane, existing fruit trees left in place. Overcast soft daylight, realistic, quiet, small scale, the house is clearly subordinate to the trees. No fence, no garage, no dormers.

**Deutsch (Kurz):** Blick vom Grenzweg auf die geschlossene Nordfassade: Haustür, ein Küchenfenster, ein kleines Badfenster, Fallrohr in Regentonne, Kiesweg, Schotterrasen-Stellplatz, Haus tritt hinter den Bäumen zurück.

## Prompt D – Terrasse am Abend (Stimmungsbild)

> Blue hour architectural photograph. Small modern timber cabin with a steep 45° gable roof in dark standing-seam metal, silver-grey horizontal larch cladding, at the edge of a forest. The large floor-to-ceiling sliding glass door on the long side is open, warm light glows from inside showing a compact interior with pale spruce panelling, a small kitchen and a wood stove. A low larch deck, 3 m deep, without railing, with two simple chairs and a lantern. Mist over the meadow, dark silhouettes of trees, a few stars. Extremely calm, Nordic cabin mood, photorealistic, 35 mm, no people.

## Prompt E – Innenraum (Wohnen/Kochen mit Blick auf die Wiese)

> Interior photograph of a very small modern cabin, 6.6 m × 4.6 m interior, single open room: living, dining and kitchen. Walls and the steep 45° pitched ceiling are lined with pale whitewashed spruce boards, exposed timber rafters, oak floor. A full-height sliding glass door opens onto a larch deck and a meadow with an autumn forest behind. Simple kitchen along the back wall in light wood with a dark stone worktop, small dining table for four, a compact wood stove, a ladder to a small sleeping loft under the roof. Daylight, warm minimal Scandinavian interior, photorealistic, 24 mm lens, no people.

## Prompt F – Variante B (8,40 × 6,80 m) als Vergleichsbild

Prompt B verwenden und ersetzen: „7.2 m long, 5.2 m wide … 45° gable roof, ridge 5.3 m“ → „8.4 m long, 6.8 m wide, 38° gable roof, ridge 5.26 m above the floor, wall height 2.6 m“. Alles andere gleich lassen, damit beide Bilder direkt vergleichbar sind.

## Prompt G – Achsonometrie / Schemabild für die Präsentation (weißes Modell)

> Clean architectural axonometric illustration, white model style with subtle shadows on a light background. A small gable-roofed cabin 7.2 × 5.2 m with a 45° roof, a 3 m deep deck on the long south side, a dashed rectangle on the ground marking the building envelope exactly under the house, a small parking space and a gravel path to the north, a forest edge indicated with simple round tree symbols on the east side. Minimal, diagrammatic, thin dark lines, light wood and dark grey roof as the only colours, no text.

## Fixdaten (an jeden Prompt anhängen)

> Fixed facts, do not change: footprint 7.2 m × 5.2 m (Variant A) / 8.4 m × 6.8 m (Variant B); one storey only; symmetrical gable roof, 45° (A) / 38° (B), ridge parallel to the long side; ridge height 5.3 m above floor, wall height 2.7 m; eave overhang 0.4 m, gable overhang 0.3 m; matte anthracite standing-seam metal roof (RAL 7016), not zinc, not copper; horizontal untreated larch cladding, silver-grey; dark grey window frames; large glazing only on the south (meadow) side; small windows on the other sides; timber deck 3 m deep at floor level, no railing; building raised 30 cm on hidden point footings; no dormers, no chimney stack (only a small dark flue pipe if any), no balcony, no basement, no garage, no fence, no white render, no glossy surfaces.

**Negativ-Prompt (falls das Tool einen hat):** dormer, chimney, balcony, second floor, garage, carport, white render, brick, red roof tiles, glossy metal, copper, zinc patina, fence, cars, people, text, watermark, oversized house, flat roof, log cabin, rustic round logs.

---

## Tipps für die Präsentation

- Bilder als **„Visualisierung, unverbindlich“** beschriften und nie ohne die Maßangaben (Blatt 3–5) zeigen; die Gemeinde prüft Firsthöhe und Baufenster, nicht die Stimmung.
- Ein Bild vom **Bestand** daneben stellen (dein Drohnenfoto ohne Haus), damit der Vergleich vorher/nachher sichtbar ist.
- Bei Variante B ehrlich zeigen, wie weit das Haus über das Baufenster hinausgeht (Isometrie Blatt 10 zeigt das gestrichelte Baufenster unter dem Haus).
